# Campaigns, Calendars, Copy Formulas & Hook Bank

Use this for anything strategic or multi-post: "a week of content," "a launch," "a
promo," "a campaign." For one-offs you usually just need the hook bank + formulas.

## The content mix (so the feed doesn't feel like an ad wall)

Rotate these five angles. A healthy week hits most of them:

1. **Pain-point** — name the owner's problem hard. ("No-shows again? Your chair earns
   $0 when it's empty.")
2. **Feature-in-action** — show a ClipWise screen solving it. ("Deposits collected
   automatically. Watch.")
3. **Proof / stat** — a number or outcome. ("Shops on ClipWise fill more chairs. Here's
   how." — use only truthful figures; ask if you need a real one.)
4. **Offer / CTA** — the free plan, Pro price, a demo. ("Free forever plan. Start today
   at clipwise.ca.")
5. **Social proof / education** — a testimonial (real only), a tip, "how top shops run
   their books." Builds trust and reach.

Ratio of ~3 value : 1 promo keeps followers around while still converting.

## Campaign playbooks

### Product / feature launch
1. **Tease** (1–2 posts): "Something's coming to make payday painless." Blue glow,
   mystery, no reveal.
2. **Reveal** (hero post + reel): the feature, the outcome, a product-screen demo.
3. **Educate** (carousel + YouTube demo): how it works, step by step.
4. **Convert** (offer post + retarget ad): CTA to start free / book a demo.
5. **Reinforce** (testimonial / stat): proof it works. Ask the owner for a real quote.

### Seasonal / promo (e.g. "New Year, new books")
- Tie ClipWise to the owner's calendar: New Year (get organized), tax season (tips &
  tax handled), summer rush (waitlist/kiosk), holidays (gift cards!). Gift cards +
  loyalty are natural Q4/holiday hooks.
- Always: real dates, real terms. If the promo has a discount/deadline, get the exact
  numbers from the owner before designing.

### Feature-spotlight series (evergreen, low effort)
- One recurring template, one feature per week: booking, POS, payments/tips, analytics,
  loyalty, gift cards, no-show protection, payroll, inventory, waitlist, kiosk.
- Build the template once (Figma/Canva) and swap copy + screenshot each week.

## Copy formulas

**Headline / hook formulas** (first 3 words must stop the scroll):
- *Problem → twist:* "Empty chair. Full stress."
- *Cost of inaction:* "Every no-show is a haircut you'll never get paid for."
- *Before/after:* "From sticky notes to one dashboard."
- *Number-led:* "$29.99/mo. Everything to run your shop."
- *Question:* "Still chasing tips at closing time?"
- *Command:* "Run your shop like a pro."

**Caption skeleton:**
```
[Hook line — the scroll-stopper.]

[1–2 lines: the problem, then how ClipWise fixes it. Concrete, benefit-led.]

[CTA — one, clear.] Start free at clipwise.ca 💈

[Optional: 1 line of personality / question to drive comments.]
```

**CTA bank:** "Start free — no card at clipwise.ca" · "Book a 10-min demo" · "Try the
free plan today" · "Link in bio 💈" · "DM us 'DEMO'".

## Hook bank by feature (steal these)

- **No-shows / deposits:** "Your chair sat empty. Your bank account noticed." · "No
  deposit, no problem? Wrong." · "Turn 'sorry I forgot' into money in the bank."
- **Payments / tips / tax:** "Card, cash, online — every tip tracked to the cent." ·
  "Tax season shouldn't be a shoebox of receipts." · "Get paid the second the cut ends."
- **Payroll / commission:** "Payday math shouldn't take all night." · "Commission,
  calculated. You just cut hair."
- **Analytics:** "Guessing isn't a business plan." · "Your best day, your best barber,
  your best service — one screen."
- **Loyalty / gift cards:** "Regulars are gold. Reward them in seconds." · "Sell gift
  cards while you sleep."
- **Booking / kiosk / waitlist:** "Booked while you were mid-fade." · "Walk-ins,
  organized. Nobody storms out." · "Your booking page never sleeps."
- **Free plan / pricing:** "Free forever. Yes, actually." · "$29.99/mo. Cancel the
  chaos, not the plan."

Pick ~10 that fit the specific post; don't paste all of them. Rotate to avoid
shadow-ban patterns. Put them in the caption or first comment.

## Truthfulness guardrail

Never invent stats, reviews, customer names, or results. Real numbers we can always
use: the free plan exists, Pro is $29.99/mo, the feature list is real. For anything
else specific ("shops saw +X%," a named testimonial), ask the owner for the real
figure/quote before putting it in content.
