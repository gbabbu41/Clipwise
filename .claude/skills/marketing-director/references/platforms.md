# Platform Specs & Playbook

Get dimensions right the FIRST time — a mis-sized asset gets cropped or rejected and
wastes the owner's upload. All sizes in pixels (W×H). When one concept spans platforms,
design the tallest/most-constrained version first, then reframe with `video_resize` /
Canva resize / a re-export rather than redrawing.

## Instagram (primary — most barbershop owners live here)

| Asset | Size | Ratio | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Feed post (portrait) | 1080×1350 | 4:5 | **Default feed choice** — takes the most screen height. |
| Feed post (square) | 1080×1080 | 1:1 | Safe, but 4:5 outperforms. |
| Reel / Story | 1080×1920 | 9:16 | Keep text/logo inside the center; top ~250px & bottom ~320px get covered by UI. |
| Carousel | 1080×1350 each | 4:5 | 3–7 slides. Slide 1 = hook, last = CTA. |
| Profile picture | 320×320 shown, upload 1080×1080 | 1:1 | Renders as a circle — keep the mark centered, no corners. |

- **Reels are the growth engine** — prioritize them. Hook in first 1s, on-screen
  captions always (most watch muted), 7–30s, one takeaway, CTA end card.
- **Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week, at least 2 reels. Stories daily-ish (low effort).
- **Best times (ET, Canada):** Tue–Thu 11am–1pm and 7–9pm; Reels do well early
  evening. (Refine once real analytics exist.)
- Hashtags: 8–15 in the caption or first comment.

## Facebook (owner communities, older barbers, local groups)

| Asset | Size | Ratio | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Feed image | 1200×630 | ~1.91:1 | Landscape for link posts. |
| Feed portrait | 1080×1350 | 4:5 | For native image posts. |
| Reel / Story | 1080×1920 | 9:16 | Cross-post the IG reel. |
| Cover photo | 1640×624 (safe area narrower) | — | Keep text centered; mobile crops the sides. |
| Profile picture | 1080×1080 | 1:1 | Same as IG, circular crop. |
| Ad (single image) | 1080×1080 or 1200×628 | 1:1 / 1.91:1 | Keep text light; Meta favors <20% text. |

- Great for **link posts to clipwise.ca**, longer captions, and Groups
  (barbershop-owner communities). Value posts > pure promo here.
- **Cadence:** 3–4/week. **Best times:** weekdays 9–11am & 1–3pm.

## YouTube (demos, tutorials, "how I run my shop" — high-intent buyers)

| Asset | Size | Ratio | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Thumbnail | 1280×720 | 16:9 | Big bold text (≤5 words), one face/screen, high contrast. Thumbnail = the click. |
| Video (standard) | 1920×1080 | 16:9 | Demos, walkthroughs, testimonials. |
| Short | 1080×1920 | 9:16 | Repurpose IG reels; add "Shorts" hook. |
| Channel banner | 2560×1440 (safe area 1546×423 center) | — | Keep logo + tagline in the center safe zone. |
| Channel profile pic | 800×800 | 1:1 | Circular. |

- YouTube is where owners go to *evaluate* — lean into **product demos and
  tutorials** ("Set up online booking in 3 minutes"), testimonials, comparisons.
- Thumbnails matter more than the video for CTR — always design a custom one.
- **Cadence:** 1 long video/week is plenty; Shorts as often as you can repurpose.

## TikTok (younger barbers, viral reach — optional but high upside)

## Universal rules

- **Safe zones:** on 9:16, keep essential text/logo in the middle 60% vertically.
- **Muted-first:** assume no sound on video — burn in captions.
- **First frame / first line does the work.** The hook earns everything after it.
- **One CTA**, and make it real: "Start free at clipwise.ca."
- **Export quality:** PNG for graphics with text/flat color; JPG only for photos; MP4
  (H.264) for video. Deliver at native resolution, not upscaled.
- When unsure which platform, default to the **IG 4:5 feed post + a 9:16 reel** pair —
  that covers IG + FB + can be trimmed for the rest.
